Your data on this device

Your records are saved in this browser on this device using local storage. PeriodPal does not send them anywhere. Local storage is not encrypted health-data storage, so anyone who can use this browser on this device may be able to see your records. Clearing your browser's site data or history may also erase them.

Understand Your Cycle

Cycle length is measured from the first day of one period to the first day of the next. Cycles vary from person to person.

Privacy First

PeriodPal does not send your information anywhere. Your records are saved in this browser on this device using local storage, which is not encrypted. Anyone who can use this browser may be able to see them, and clearing your browser data can erase them.

About PeriodPal

PeriodPal is designed to help you record menstrual dates and become more aware of your own cycle patterns. This early version saves your records in this browser, works out your cycle lengths, shows a calendar and history, and gives an estimated next start date.

PeriodPal provides date-recording tools and educational information, not medical advice. Menstrual cycles vary, and future dates cannot be predicted with certainty.
